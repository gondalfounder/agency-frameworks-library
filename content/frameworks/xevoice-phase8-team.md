We've built a really cool AI receptionist system. I'm part of the founding team here, and I just wanted to set up a quick call to show you something interesting. Do you have 10 minutes this week?"*

## INTERN(S) — Execution Interns

**Framework Reference: Arham Abid + Shadab Omer (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### When to Hire Interns

- Jab Shehryar ya Farzan ka 20%+ time boring repetitive tasks pe ja raha ho
- Jab lead scraping bottleneck ban raha ho
- Jab content uploading delay ho rahi ho

**Compensation:** Unpaid (learning opportunity) ya minimum stipend (5,000–10,000 PKR/month)

**Source:** University students — AI, Marketing, CS backgrounds preferred
