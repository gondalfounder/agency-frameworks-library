## RULE #0 — Pricing Philosophy: Value-Based, Never Cost-Based

**Framework: Saad Munir (Agency Building in 2026)**

**Wrong Way ("Majboori" / Desperation Pricing):**
- Client aaya → koi bhi price accept karo taake deal close ho
- Tools ki cost pe price karo (e.g. "Vapi costs $X so I'll charge $X+10")
- Panic mein discount do

**Correct Pricing Equation (Saad Munir):**
> Price = Exact Operational Costs + Value-Based Profit Margins

**Value drivers:**
- Lead generation channel (cold call = low trust = lower entry price; organic = high trust = premium)
- Founder positioning aur authority
- Client ka revenue potential (how much you save/make them)

**Pansari Mentality Warning (Saad Munir):**
Incoming client cash seedha personal spending pe mat lagao — yeh agency destroy kar deta hai. Har deal ka pehle operational cost calculate karo, phir margin lo, phir salary lo.

## RULE #1 — "Recurring is Growth" — Kabhi One-Time Setup Model Nahi

**Framework: Carmel Hernandez + Saad Munir (AI Voice Agency Resources + Agency Building in 2026)**

**Fatal Flaw of Setup-Only Model:**
- $2,000–$5,000 setup fee → agent hand-off → HIGH CHURN
- Non-technical clients cannot monitor prompts, fix edge cases, manage APIs
- They email/text for months → think product failed → leave
- Agla mahina: **$0 revenue**

*Carmel Hernandez: "You would not take a corporate 9-to-5 job if your salary was $10K one month and $1K the next."*

**XeVoice Standard Model:**
- Setup fee: **lower or $0 upfront**
- Monthly retainer: **$1,000–$2,000/month**
- Commitment: **3–6 month contract minimum**

**Delivery Curve:**
- Months 1–2: Heavy setup, prompt tuning, CRM integration, edge case fixing
- Month 3+: Agent runs smoothly → minimal effort → pure recurring margin

### Service 1 Add-On: Usage-Based Per-Minute Model

**Framework: Jeremy Camilloni (AI Voice Agency Resources) — 90% Margin Model**

**Raw tech stack cost:** $0.09–$0.12/minute (Vapi/Retell + LLM + Transcriber)

**XeVoice client charge:** $0.95–$1.00/minute

**Math:**
- 10x markup on raw software costs
- **90% profit margin**

**Minimum tier:** 100 minutes = ~$95–$100/month (never go below)

**Automated Overage Billing:**
- Backend dashboard tracks usage per billing cycle → connected to Stripe
- Client agreement: *"If we go over, you get prorated at $0.95/min"*
- No renegotiation needed

**Volume Scaling Advantage:**
- Low entry: $95/month → client never cancels
- Client's business grows or seasonal spike → billing auto-scales to $300–$500+/month
- *"Bring them on for low rate — as they grow, you bill on volume"* — Jeremy Camilloni

**What per-minute price includes (bundle this in pitch):**
- Prompt optimization
- Call log monitoring
- Agent retraining on live edge cases
- Proactive maintenance
- White-glove management

## STEP 2: Channel-Based Pricing Architecture

**Framework: Shadab Omer (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

> **Critical Rule:** Agar main offer hai aur subset offers nahi hain — bahut badi galti kar rahe ho.

| Traffic Channel | Trust Level | XeVoice Pricing |
|----------------|------------|----------------|

*"He just met you 4 hours ago on a call. He doesn't know you enough to give you that."*

## STEP 3: Entry Offers & Risk Reversal

**Framework: Kip Hernandez + Arham Abid + Saad Munir**

### Option A — Free 30-Day Proof-of-Concept (Zero Social Proof Stage)

**Framework: Kip Hernandez**
- Client: sirf raw software usage cost cover kare
- Agency: full build + monitoring kare FREE
- Exchange: honest 5-minute Riverside video testimonial
- Conversion: 10–20 pitched → 5 yes → 3 become paying retainer clients

**When to use:** Jab koi case study ya testimonial nahi ho (early stage).

### Option B — $297 XeVoice Revenue Leak Audit

**Framework: Saad Munir**
- "30-minute AI Revenue Audit — kitne calls miss ho rahe hain, kitna revenue leak ho raha hai"
- If audit purchased → $297 full setup fee mein credit ho jaata hai
- *"Ek dafa card bahar aa jaye — upsell bahut aasaan ho jaata hai"*

### Option C — Low-Barrier Database Reactivation

**Framework: Arham Abid**
- $100–$200/month automated text/voice reactivation campaign on client's old database
- Immediate ROI deliver karo → long-term retainer mein upsell karo
- Guarantee: *"1.5x your revenue in 60 days — if not, you don't pay"*

### Option D — Pay-Per-Show (Cold Channel Only)

**Framework: Shadab Omer**
- Rate: $400–$500 per confirmed show-up / booked appointment
- Best for high-ticket clients: Oral Surgeons ($5K–$80K per patient)
- Never: Pay-Per-Close, Commission, Revenue Share

**Why NEVER Pay-Per-Close (Shadab Omer):**
1. Client ke private books ka access nahi → rely on their honesty
2. In-office closing = client's skill, not yours → not your responsibility
*"Jo cheez mere control mein nahi — uski responsibility main nahi le sakta."*

## STEP 5: Pricing Objection Handling Scripts

**Framework: Afroz Khan + Saad Munir (Agency Building in 2026)**

### "Price Is Too High" Objection

**First — Ask (Afroz Khan):**
> *"Compared to what?"*

Uncover whether: genuine lack of capital, poor ICP targeting, or lack of trust.

**Then — Cost of Inaction Frame (Saad Munir + Carmel Hernandez):**

**Live Calculation on Call:**
> *"You mentioned you miss about 7–8 calls per day. If 5 of those would have booked at $200 average job — that's $1,000/day, $30,000/month in lost revenue. Our system at $500/month captures that. The cost of inaction is always higher than the cost of action."*

*Saad Munir: "Yeh sentence mera favorite hai — mein 400 calls mein use kar chuka hun."*

### "I Need to Think About It" Objection

**Script (Afroz Khan):**
> *"Usually when people say they need to think about it, it means they either aren't completely certain about the system, or the price feels out of reach. Which one is it for you?"*

### "I'll Talk to My Partner" Objection

**Prevention during Discovery (Afroz Khan):**
> *"Besides yourself, is there anyone else involved in approving new growth systems for your company?"*

**If objection occurs:**
> *"That makes total sense. Let's schedule a 15-minute call with both of you so I can walk through the ROI numbers directly."*

**NEVER:** Email PDF proposal for them to review alone — proposals without a call rarely close.

### 1-to-10 Pre-Price Framework (Alex Hormozi — Referenced by Saad Munir + Afroz Khan)

Price batane se PEHLE run karo:

**Step 1:**
> *"On a scale of 1 to 10 — 1 being complete BS and 10 being exactly what you need — how close does this system feel to your goals?"*

**Step 2 (If they say 8):** "Why not a 1?" — NOT "Why not a 10?"
- Forces them to list all benefits themselves → self-sells

**Step 3:**
> *"What would it take to turn that 8 into a 10?"*
- Hidden objections surface BEFORE price drop

**Step 4 — Price Drop:**
> *"The investment to get you from where you are now to where you want to go is [Tier X]."*

**Step 5 — SILENT. 5–10 seconds. Do NOT speak first.**

## STEP 6: Price Withholding — Two-Call Close Rule

**Framework: Carmel Hernandez (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

**Call 1 (Discovery):**
- Price KABHI mat batao
- *"Everything we build is fully custom — I need to consult our technical team and create a tailored proposal for you."*
- No reason for them to back out — they don't even know the price

**Call 2 (Custom Proposal):**
- Use their exact words from Call 1
- Show calculated revenue loss (missed calls × average job value)
- Present 3 tailored options
- Now you close

**Why 2-Call Works (Carmel Hernandez):**
*"There's a statistic that someone needs to spend about 7 hours with you before purchasing. Two calls + email sequence + testimonial videos gets them there."*

## STEP 7: When to Raise Prices

**Framework: Furqan Aziz + Saad Munir (Agency Building in 2026)**

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Net profit per client below target | Raise rate on next NEW prospect (not existing) |
| Delivering consistent results + client happy | Upsell to next tier |
| $5K/month crossed | Begin transitioning to niche-specific pricing |
| New case study / testimonial secured | Increase standard rates by 20–30% |

**Rate Increment Sequence for XeVoice:**
- Launch: $350/month (Starter)
- → $500/month (after first 3 clients)
- → $700/month (after first case study)
- → $1,000+/month (after niche-specific positioning)

**Non-Negotiable Rule (Saad Munir):**
- Legacy clients ka rate KABHI mat badhao
- Nayi leads pe incrementally higher rate test karo
- Extra margin goes to company reserves, NOT personal spending

**Capital Allocation Order — Non-Negotiable (Saad Munir):**

- Reinvest profits into: paid communities, masterminds, networking events, tools, team

- $10K/month target dekhke paralysis hoti hai
- Pehla goal: **extra $100/week**
- Pehla $100 aate hi: model validate → momentum build → next step
