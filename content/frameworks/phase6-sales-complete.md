## 1. Sales Psychology & Core Mindset

**Source: Saad Munir, Afroz Khan (Agency Building in 2026)**

### Sales as an Exchange of Energy (Saad Munir + Afroz Khan)

Sales = coercion ya manipulation nahi — yeh **exchange of energy** hai. Closer ek trusted advisor ki tarah act karta hai jo prospect ko painful business problem solve karne ki taraf guide karta hai.

**Energy Shift Rule:**
High-ticket deals ($2,000–$50,000+) desperation se nahi — **helpful authority** se close hoti hain. Jab tum "sell karna" band karo aur focus karo sirf yeh diagnose karne pe ke prospect fit hai ya nahi — closing rates dramatically increase hoti hain.

### "Order Taking" Trap (Afroz Khan, Saad Munir)

**The Problem:**
Upwork/Fiverr founders sales call pe aate hain aur seedha client ko dictate karne dete hain — *"I need a website with X features, Python backend, custom dashboard"* — notes likhte hain jaise restaurant waiter.

**The Flaw:**
Order-taking = false sense of progress + positioning destroy + agency commoditized + extreme price sensitivity.

**The Fix:**
Closer ko frame control maintain karna hai, structured diagnostic questions se conversation lead karni hai, aur solution **prescribe** karna hai — client-prescribed orders accept nahi karne.

### Consultation Call vs. Sales Call (Afroz Khan)

| Type | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Consultation Call** | Technical advice dena, problem on-the-spot solve karna, prospect ko free educate karna |
| **Sales Call** | Structured conversation — prospect ko **Cost of Inaction** feel karwana jab tak agency hire nahi karta |

**Rule:** Sales call pe consultation mat karo. Diagnose karo, cost calculate karwao, solution pitch karo.

### Tonality & The Friendliness Trap (Shadab Omer)

**Warning:** Prospect ke saath over-friendly ho jaana (weather, sports, personal life) = **"Fake Reality"** create ho jaati hai.
- Prospect tum se "friend" ki tarah like karta hai lekin transact karne mein hesitate karta hai
- Favors maangne lagte hain, delays karte hain: *"Will talk to my people and come back to you"*
- Agle calls pe football ki baatein shuru ho jaati hain — deal nahi hota

**Correct Approach (Shadab Omer):**
*"Main bahut transactional hun — ek acha tareeqe se. Mujhe paise banana hai, lekin aapko bhi zyada paise banana hain. Tab hi mujhe paise milte hain."*

Strictly **transactionally focused in a good way** — mutual revenue generation aur business problems solving pe.

## 2. Pre-Call Assets, Qualification & BUNFEM

**Source: Afroz Khan, Saad Munir (Agency Building in 2026)**

### Pre-Framing & Pre-Call Assets (Afroz Khan + Saad Munir)

Sales call se **24–48 hours pehle** bhejo:
- Short **Video Sales Letter (VSL)**
- Case study breakdown PDF
- Pre-call landing page with client video testimonials

**Impact:** Basic questions answer ho jaate hain, authority establish, prospect enters call pre-sold.

### BUNFEM Qualification Framework (Saad Munir + Afroz Khan)

Senior closer ka time allocate karne se pehle prospect ko qualify karo:

| Letter | Stands For | Check Karo |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| **B** | Budget | Prospect ke paas baseline retainer afford karne ki financial capacity hai? |
| **U** | Urgency | Yeh problem abhi solve karne ki pressing reason hai? |
| **N** | Need | Clear, quantifiable operational ya revenue gap exist karta hai? |
| **F** | Fit | Prospect tumhari agency ke delivery SOPs se match karta hai? |
| **E** | Expectations | Unki timeline aur output expectations realistic hain? |
| **M** | Meeting | **Book a Meeting From an Other Meeting** — next step non-negotiable lock karo |

**Minimum Qualification Benchmarks:**
- $1M+ ARR (mid-tier agency ke liye)
- 5+ attorneys (law practices ke liye)
- 3+ crews (home contractors ke liye)
- Active, clear pain point

## 3. Complete 7-Stage Sales Call Architecture

**Source: Afroz Khan, Saad Munir (Agency Building in 2026) — Drawing from Alex Hormozi, Jeremy Miner's NEPQ, Eli Wilde**

| Stage | Duration | Focus |
|-------|---------|-------|
| **Stage 1** | 2–3 mins | Rapport Building & Relatability |
| **Stage 2** | 2 mins | Setting the Frame & Agenda |
| **Stage 3** | 15–20 mins | Discovery & Deep Discovery |
| **Stage 4** | 5 mins | Pain Amplification & Cost of Inaction |
| **Stage 5** | 5 mins | Selling the Dream / Outcome |
| **Stage 6** | Max 10 mins | The Pitch & Unique Mechanism |
| **Stage 7** | 10 mins | Pre-Price Certainty (1-to-10) & Price Drop |

### Stage 1: Rapport Building (2–3 Minutes)

- Prospect ki energy level se match karo immediately
- Icebreakers brief, natural, aur relatable rakho
- Artificial small talk avoid karo — warm, professional connection jaldi establish karo

### Stage 2: Setting the Frame & Agenda (2 Minutes)

Meeting structure define karo, time boundaries set karo, decision-making expectation upfront establish karo.

**Script (Afroz Khan):**
> *"Hey [Prospect Name], this call is scheduled for [X] minutes. We're going to discuss the specific bottlenecks in your business, see what's working, what's not, and determine whether we're the right fit to help you fix this. If at the end of the call I feel we can genuinely help you reach your goal, we'll discuss how we can move forward today. If not, I'll point you in the right direction. Does that sound fair?"*

### Stage 3: Discovery & Deep Discovery (15–20 Minutes)

**The 60/40 Rule (Saad Munir + Afroz Khan):**
- Prospect: **60% of the time bole**
- Closer: **40% ya kam bole**
- Diagnostic questions force karte hain prospect ko apna pain verbalize aur internalize karne pe

**Initial Discovery Questions:**
> *"What was the primary reason you booked this call today?"*
> *"How long has this been an issue for your business?"*
> *"What else have you tried to solve this? Have you hired an agency or built an in-house team previously?"*

**Deep Discovery:** Surface-level symptoms nahi — hidden operational root causes uncover karo.

### Stage 4: Pain Amplification & Calculating Financial Loss

**The Rule:** Prospect ki operational gap se exact dollar loss quantify karo.

**Live Calculation Example (Afroz Khan):**
- Closer: *"What happens if you miss calls during peak hours?"*
- Prospect: *"People call another place."*
- Closer: *"How many calls do you lose per day?"*
- Prospect: *"About 7 to 8 calls a day."*
- Closer calculates LIVE: *"If 5 of those 8 calls would have booked, with average ticket of $150, you're losing $750/day. That's over $22,000 every single month."*

### Stage 5: Selling the Dream / Outcome

**The Rule (Afroz Khan):** KABHI MAT BECHO:
- Technical tools
- AI scripts
- Coding languages
- Marketing tactics (Meta pining, tags, API keys)

**HAMESHA BECHO:**
- Desired **outcome** (more profit, faster time-to-market)
- **Risk mitigation** (bad developers se bachna, ad spend leakage rokna, compliance fines avoid karna)

**Two Reasons Buyers Invest:**
1. Specific commercial outcome achieve karna
2. Major risk mitigate karna

**VSL Framework:** Vision → Friction → Lift

### Stage 6: The Pitch & Unique Mechanism (Max 10 Minutes)

- Presentation **10 minutes se zyada nahi**
- Har feature ko micro-case study, proof screenshot, ya visual asset se directly relate karo
- Solution ko **Unique Mechanism** mein frame karo:
  - e.g. *"The 3-Step Sync Blueprint: Diagnose → Install → Optimize"*
  - e.g. *"The 60-Day Scalability Blueprint"*

### Stage 7: Pre-Price Certainty — Alex Hormozi's 1-to-10 Framework

Price batane se PEHLE hidden objections surface karo.

**Step 1 — The Question:**
> *"Based on everything we've covered today, on a scale of 1 to 10 — 1 being complete BS and 10 being exactly what you need — how close does this system feel to getting you to your goal?"*

**Step 2 — Counter-Intuitive Follow-Up:**
Prospect "8" bole → "Why not a 10?" MAT pucho.
Pucho: **"Why not a 1?"**
- Psychological Effect: Prospect khud apne fayde list karne lagta hai — offer ko self-sells karta hai

**Step 3 — Isolate the Gap:**
> *"What would it take to turn that 8 into a 10?"*
- Hidden objections price batane se pehle surface hoti hain
- Certainty ya scope concerns pehle resolve karo → phir price batao

**Step 4 — Price Drop:**
3-tier structure clearly state karo → **completely silent raho 5–10 seconds** jab tak prospect numbers process kare.

## 4. Two-Call Close Framework

**Source: Carmel Hernandez (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### Call 1 — Discovery & Live Proof (1 Hour)

**Focus:** Deep discovery — call volume, pain points, missed lead gaps, lost revenue calculate karo.

**Show:** Live client call recordings ya custom builds.

**Price:** KABHI MAT BATAO.
> *"Everything we do is fully custom, so I need to consult our technical team and build a tailored proposal."*

**Why:** Pricing withhold karna ensure karta hai ke prospect Call 2 pe show kare bina back out kiye.

### Call 2 — Custom Proposal Presentation (1 Hour)

**Preparation:** Prospect ke exact phrasing use karo, unka calculated revenue loss dikhao, tailored ROI options present karo.

**Why it closes easier:** Trust establish ho chuka hai, solution unke apne words mein frame kiya gaya hai.

**Buyer Psychology (Carmel Hernandez):**
- B2B business owners cautious hote hain — software companies aur agencies ne pehle burn kiya hai
- Research: B2B buyers ko high-ticket purchasing decisions ke liye roughly **7 hours of interaction/content consumption** chahiye
- Two-call structure = required touchpoints for high-ticket commitment

## 5. Bait-and-Switch Cold Sales Formula

**Source: Shadab Omer (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

**Step 1 — The Bait:**
Free 10-minute strategy session ya audit offer karo based on past success:
> *"How we generated $1M for a dental practice — let me show you how we did it."*

**Step 2 — The Free Asset:**
Complete growth strategy free mein Zoom call pe lay out karo.

**Step 3 — The Switch:**
Backend labor ka complexity reveal karo:
- Speed-to-lead calls
- GHL setup
- Booking management
- Rescheduling, cancellation handling
- Call monitoring

Phir full **Done-For-You (DFY)** package mein transition karo.

**Selling Angle (Shadab Omer):**
> *"You don't pay me anything unless I make you money. I don't make money if you don't make money."*

## 7. Objection Handling — Complete Framework

**Source: Afroz Khan, Saad Munir (Agency Building in 2026) + Carmel Hernandez, Shadab Omer (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### 2 Categories of Objections (Shadab Omer)

| Type | Examples | Real Reason |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **Fear-Based (Smoke Screen)** | "I need to talk to my wife," "Price is too high," "Let me think about it" | Prospect fears wasting time ya money — yeh real reason nahi |
| **Logistical (Real Obstacles)** | "Our ad account is disabled," "We use franchise system," "Call back in 3 months" | Real operational barriers — technical solutions chahiye |

**Key Insight (Shadab Omer):** Fear-based objections mein interest toh hota hai — sirf uncertainty hai. Logistical mein real barrier hai jo solve karni hai. Dono alag tareeqe se handle hoti hain.

### "I Need to Think About It" (Afroz Khan)

**Galti mat karo:** *"Okay, let me know when you decide."*

**Correct Response:**
> *"I completely understand. Usually when people say they need to think about it, it means they either aren't completely certain about the system, or the price feels out of reach. Which one is it for you?"*

**Cost of Inaction Frame:**
> *"You mentioned earlier that unhandled leads are costing your business roughly $3,000 every single month ($36,000/year). Compared to losing $36,000 year after year, a $5,000 investment to fix this today is minimal. Is delaying this another month worth losing another $3,000?"*

**Master Closing Anchor Line (Afroz Khan):**
> *"The cost of inaction is always higher than the cost of action."*

*Saad Munir: "Yeh sentence mera favorite hai — mein 400 calls mein use kar chuka hun."*

### "I Need to Talk to My Partner / Spouse" (Afroz Khan)

**Prevention during Discovery:**
> *"Besides yourself, is there anyone else involved in approving new growth systems for the company?"*

**If objection occurs:**
- Email proposal kabhi mat bhejo for them to review alone — proposals bina call ke rarely close hoti hain

**Correct Response:**
> *"That makes total sense. Since your partner hasn't seen the architecture or the case studies we reviewed today, let's schedule a brief 15-minute call with both of you. I can walk through the exact roadmap and answer any technical questions directly."*

### "Price is Too High / No Budget" (Afroz Khan)

Ask: **"Compared to what?"**

Uncover whether:
- Genuine lack of capital
- Poor ICP targeting (prospect qualify hi nahi tha)
- Lack of trust/certainty in deliverable

### Handling "Driving / Busy" Objection (Carmel Hernandez)

> *"I know you're driving right now, so I'll be really quick... Can I put a Google Meet invite tentatively in your calendar? I just sent it over — if you could just accept that for me while on the phone so I know it's in my calendar as well, then we should be good."*

### Handling "We Have Dispatchers / Don't Miss Calls" (Carmel Hernandez)

> *"That's great you have dispatchers. We custom build AI solutions specifically for after-hours calls or overflow when your dispatchers are busy or on other lines, middle of the night, so zero revenue falls through the cracks."*

### Handling "We're Part of a Franchise" (Carmel Hernandez)

> *"No, that makes sense. Do they handle the call center centrally, or do you manage your own local incoming calls and leads? ... We've had many clients come to us from having used generic corporate options because they weren't customized to their specific office. We custom build it from the ground up for your location and handle outbound follow-ups for past customers."*

### Calling Out Inattentive Prospects (Shadab Omer)

Agar prospect distracted, unengaged, ya disrespectful ho:

> *"Is this a good time? Because we can always reschedule if I caught you at a bad time. I want your attention because this might change the direction of your business or save you lost revenue."*

**Effect:** Authority flip — 0% se real close chance mein convert. *"Jo deal vaise bhi nahi honi thi — us mein 1% chance bhi aa jaye, worth it hai."*

## 8. Follow-Up Protocol & BUNFEM

**Source: Afroz Khan, Saad Munir (Agency Building in 2026)**

### BUNFEM Rule (Book a Meeting From an Other Meeting)

**Rule:** Kisi bhi prospect ke saath koi bhi call KABHI bina BUNFEM ke end mat karo.

- Live calendar pe exact date, time, aur invite lock karo call pe hi
- *"I'll email you next week"* = deal killer
- Always: *"Before we end — let's lock in the next touchpoint right now."*

### "No Async Proposal" Rule (Afroz Khan)

- Offline PDF proposals via email → extremely low close rates
- Proposal zaroori ho toh: 4 days baad send karo ya **live 15-minute Proposal Review Call** pe review karo
- *"Proposals sent without a call rarely close."*

### Automated Pre-Call Nurture Sequence (Kip Hernandez)

Jab lead calendar pe book kare → automatically email sequence trigger karo:
- Video testimonials
- Client case studies
- Performance stats

Purpose: Prospect ko excited rakho, no-shows combat karo, pre-sell karo.

## 9. Value Equation Scripting Framework

**Source: Shadab Omer (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

All sales scripts is formula ke around structure karo:

> Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)

**Application in Scripts:**
- **Dream Outcome maximize:** More high-ticket patients, booked jobs — specific numbers
- **Perceived Likelihood maximize:** "$1M generated for similar dental practice" — proven case study
- **Effort & Sacrifice zero:** "Done-For-You — aapko kuch nahi karna"
- **Time Delay minimize:** "30-day setup, results start month 1"

## 11. Pricing on Sales Calls (Voice Agency Specific)

**Source: Carmel Hernandez, Jeremy Camilloni, Shadab Omer (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### Price Withholding Rule (Carmel Hernandez)

- Call 1 pe price KABHI mat batao: *"Everything is custom-built."*
- Call 2 pe tailored proposal present karo.
- Yeh prevent karta hai service ko commodity mein see karne se.

### Usage-Based Per-Minute Pricing Pitch (Jeremy Camilloni)

- Raw cost: $0.09–$0.12/minute
- Client charge: $0.95–$1.00/minute (10x markup, **90% margin**)
- Minimum tier: 100 minutes = ~$95–$100/month
- Pitch: *"You're paying $2–$3/minute for human call centers. We're $0.95/minute, 24/7, fully custom — and we monitor every call."*

### Pay-Per-Show Pricing (Shadab Omer)

- **Rate:** $400–$500 per patient show-up
- **Never:** Pay-Per-Close, Commission, Revenue Share

**Why no Pay-Per-Close (Shadab Omer):**
- Client ke private financial books ka access nahi
- Unki internal sales ability tumhare control mein nahi
- *"Jo cheez mere control mein nahi — uski responsibility main nahi le sakta."*

## 12. Sales Team, Commissions & CRM

**Source: Saad Munir, Furqan Aziz, Afroz Khan (Agency Building in 2026) + Shadab Omer, Jeremy Camilloni (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### Sales Team Hierarchy

| Role | Core Responsibility |
|------|-------------------|
| **SDR / Appointment Setter** | High-volume outbound (cold calls, LinkedIn, email) → qualified appointments book karo |
| **Closer** | Discovery + closing calls → conversion drive karo |
| **Account Executive (AE)** | Client success, onboarding, relationship management, upsells |

### SDR vs. Closer Separation Rule (Saad Munir)

- SDR: High-volume cold dialing, lead filtering, appointment booking. Base salary + per-booked-appointment bonus.
- Closer: 30-minute discovery + closing calls. 100% focus on conversion.

### Utah Closer Model (Furqan Aziz)

- **Part-Time Base Salary:** $1,500–$2,000/month (US-based reps)
- **Call Capacity:** 15–20 closing calls per week

**Sliding Scale Commission (Furqan Aziz):**

| Deal Size | Commission |
|-----------|-----------|
| Small / Entry projects | 3–5% |
| Mid-ticket retainers | 7–10% |
| $1M+ Enterprise deals | 20% |

**Why sliding scale:** Closers ko high-ticket enterprise deals pursue karne ke liye incentivize karo.

### Elevated Titles Strategy (Furqan Aziz)

- Sales team members ko high-authority titles do: *Director of Business Development, VP of Engineering, Head of Growth*
- Prospect psychology: High-level executives ke saath negotiate karna prefer karta hai

**CEO Guest Appearance Hack (Furqan Aziz):**
BD rep majority of call karta hai → CEO ko 5–10 minute ke liye introduce karo:
> *"I invited our CEO to join for 10 minutes because he was excited about your project."*
High perceived value instantly.

### Master Sales Skill First Principle (Shadab Omer)

KABHI kisi ko sales outsource mat karo jab tak founder ne khud master nahi kar liya:
- Technical terminology *("ones and zeros")* samajhna zaroori hai
- Tabhi timelines manage kar sakte ho, expectations communicate kar sakte ho, lazy reps ki fabricated reports detect kar sakte ho

### Hire Beginners, Train Internally (Arham Abid)

- Expensive "highly trained" external sales executives core leadership roles mein mat hire karo
- External experts: *"They will either make you or break you — and if they break you, you can not afford that."*
- Beginners hire karo → internally train karo → loyal rahenge, culture respect karenge, growth appreciate karenge

### Nearshore SDR Hiring (Jeremy Camilloni)

- Clear English accent wale nearshore contractors hire karo low-to-medium ticket cold calling ke liye
- Currency exchange rate = attractive incentive for them
- Small business owners hang up karte hain agar non-native accent detect hon

### CRM, Pipeline & Control Tower Sheet (Saad Munir + Furqan Aziz)

**CRM Stack:**
- Single caller: **Zoom** ($5/month) — unlimited US calling + recording
- Team: **GoHighLevel (GHL)** ya **Close.com** — multi-line dialing + pipeline management

**Pipeline Stages:**
Lead Ingested → Qualified → Appointment Booked → Meeting Completed → Contract Sent → Closed Won

**Individual Profiles in CRM (Shadab Omer):**
Har SDR ke liye isolated pipeline view aur dedicated lead list assign karo — data merge nahi ho, accountability remain kare.

**Control Tower Sheet (City Sheet) — Saad Munir:**
Centralized Google Sheets/AirTable tracker:

| Status | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **Happy / Healthy** | Sales team → upsell present karo, referral maango, video testimonial record karo |
| **Curious / At-Risk** | Account Executive → scope clarify karo, support step in |
| **Super Angry** | Immediately escalate to senior leadership / CEO → churn se pehle resolve karo |
