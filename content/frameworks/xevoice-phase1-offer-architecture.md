## STEP 2: Niche Strategy — Revenue Stage ke Hisab se

**Framework: Saad Munir (Agency Building in 2026)**

### Launch Stage ($0–$5,000/month): GENERALIST RAHO

- Koi bhi local service business accept karo — plumber, HVAC, roofer, dentist
- "Dollar mat thukrao" — cash flow banao, SOPs refine karo
- Core offer sirf: **AI Voice Agent** (primary service)
- AI automation aur websites baad mein add karo

### Growth Stage ($5,000–$15,000+/month): NICHE DOWN KAO

**Saad Munir ka Formula:**
> Niche Offer = Proven Solution + Target Industry

**XeVoice ke liye:**
- "AI Voice Agents" (Solution) + "Home Service Businesses" (Industry) = **"AI Voice Agents for Home Service Businesses"**
- Ya: "AI Voice Agents" + "Dental/Medical Practices" = **"AI Receptionist for Dental Clinics"**

**ICP Selection ke Rules (Carmel Hernandez + Jeremy Camilloni):**

| Rule | Application for XeVoice |
|------|------------------------|
| **100–500+ Google Reviews** | Sirf established businesses target karo — sole operators nahi |
| **Multiple CSRs/Dispatchers** | Company itni badi ho ke call volume already high ho |
| **Owner-Operator Direct Connect** | Power washers, junk removal, landscapers — field mein khud phone uthate hain, zero gatekeepers |
| **High-Ticket Medical** | Oral Surgeons ($5K–$80K per patient) — sirf 5 clients = $40K–$45K/month |
| **Anti-Solopreneur Rule** | 1-person businesses avoid karo — "I don't want more work" objection milega |

## STEP 3: Service Packaging — XeVoice ki 3 Services kaise Bundle Karein

**Framework: Ayal & Yoav — Four Facets of Integration (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### Primary Service: AI Voice Agents

Har voice agent deployment mein yeh 4 facets include karo:

**Facet 1 — Phone System Integration:**
- Call forwarding: after-hours + overflow calls
- Warm transfers: irate callers → human staff
- Platforms: RingCentral, Vonage, Dialpad

**Facet 2 — Scripts & Brand Reflection:**
- Client ke exact tone, language, service style se match karo
- Empathy loops: stressed callers ke liye delicacy prompts
- Same training manual jo human receptionist ko dete ho

**Facet 3 — Schedule & Calendar Mapping:**
- Calendly, Cal.com, ya Google Calendar se direct integration
- Service-specific employee calendars map karo
- Cancellations ko reschedules mein convert karo

**Facet 4 — CRM & Data Structure Integration:**
- GHL, ServiceTitan, Zoho, Follow Up Boss
- Har call se data: caller name, phone, email, service, summary
- Make.com (preferred) → Zapier (fallback) → Custom REST API/Webhook

**Primary Service Outcome Terminology (Arham Abid):**

| Avoid | Use Instead |
|-------|------------|
| "AI Voice Agent" | "Never Miss a Call System" |
| "Voice AI" | "24/7 AI Receptionist that Books Jobs" |
| "Automation" | "Revenue Capture System" |

### Secondary Service: AI Automation + Customized Dashboards

**Packaging Rule (Arham Abid — AI Voice Agency Resources):**
- Core offer ke saath bundle karo: Lead Gen + CRM + Voice Follow-Up
- Dashboard = client ka "Control Tower" jahan woh results dekhe

**Avoid:**
"We build GHL automations"

**Use Instead:**
"We build a custom Revenue Dashboard that shows you every lead, every call, every booked job — in one screen"

### Third Service: AI-Powered Websites + Chatbots + AI-Based SEO

**Sales-Led SEO Framework (Umair Khalid Awan — Agency Building in 2026):**
- Website sirf banao nahi — har page ko active sales tool banao
- SEO ko brand nahi karo "SEO" — brand karo **"Search Dominance in Your Area"**
- Har text block client ka outcome sell kare (more calls, more bookings)

**Chatbot Integration:** Website chatbot + voice agent = 24/7 lead capture system

## STEP 8: 3-Tier Pricing Architecture

**Framework: Saad Munir + Kip Hernandez + Carmel Hernandez + Jeremy Camilloni**

**Non-Negotiable Rule (Carmel Hernandez):** Kabhi one-time setup fee + hand-off model mat karo. Always recurring.

### XeVoice 3-Tier Packages:

| Tier | Name | Includes | Setup | Monthly |
|------|------|---------|-------|---------|
| **Tier 1** | XeVoice Starter | 1 Inbound OR Outbound Voice Agent + Basic GHL integration + Call monitoring | $1,500 | $350–$500/month |
| **Tier 2** | XeVoice Growth | Inbound + Outbound Agents + Custom Dashboard + CRM integration + Chatbot | $3,500 | $600–$1,000/month |
| **Tier 3** | XeVoice Dominate | Full stack: Voice + Automation + Dashboard + AI Website + Chatbot + AI SEO + Multi-location | $5,000+ | $1,500–$2,500/month |

**Usage-Based Add-On (Jeremy Camilloni — 90% Margin Model):**
- Raw cost: $0.09–$0.12/minute
- Client charge: $0.95–$1.00/minute
- Minimum tier: 100 minutes = ~$95/month
- Auto-scales as business grows — Stripe automated billing
- Pitch: *"Traditional answering services charge $2–$3/minute. XeVoice is $0.95/minute, 24/7, fully managed, custom-built."*

**Commitment Term (Carmel Hernandez):** 3–6 month minimum commitment on all tiers.

## Quick Reference: XeVoice Offer Rules (What to DO / NOT DO)

| ❌ Never Do | ✅ Always Do |
|-----------|------------|
| Sell "AI Voice Agent" (jargon) | Sell "Never Miss a Job System" (outcome) |

| Niche down before $5K/month | Accept any local service client at launch |

| Rush to micro-niche too early | Macro-niche first → micro-niche at $5K+ |
| "We do SEO" | "Search Dominance in Your Area" |
| Hand-off after setup | White-glove ongoing management always |
