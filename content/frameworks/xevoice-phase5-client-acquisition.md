## STEP 1: ICP Targeting — XeVoice ke Ideal Clients

**Framework: Carmel Hernandez + Jeremy Camilloni + Shadab Omer (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### Primary ICP — Home Service Businesses

**>100–500 Google Reviews Rule (Carmel Hernandez):**
- Target: Plumbing, HVAC, Roofing, Snow Removal, Landscaping, Junk Removal
- Must have: **100–500+ Google reviews** + multiple CSRs/dispatchers
- High lead volume, missed calls daily, capital to pay retainer

**Anti-Solopreneur Rule:**
- 1-person businesses KABHI NAHI
- Objection: *"I don't want more work on my calendar"*
- No budget, no volume, no use case

**Contact Protocol:**
- HAMESHA owner ko directly call karo — private mobile number
- Main business line ya receptionist: NAHI

### Owner-Operator Direct Connect (Jeremy Camilloni)

**Best verticals — zero gatekeepers:**
- Junk removal / waste management
- Power washing
- Tree trimming
- Party buses / limo services
- Landscaping companies

**Why these work:** Owner himself field mein kaam karte hue apna phone uthata hai.

**Avoid:** Nail salons — 100 calls mein sirf ~4 decision-makers milte hain.

### Secondary ICP — High-Ticket Medical Practices

**Oral Surgeons (Shadab Omer):**
- Treatment packages: **$5,000–$80,000 per patient**
- Unit economics: 5 active clients → $40,000–$45,000/month
- Channel: Pay-Per-Show ($400–$500 per show-up)

### Tertiary ICP — White-Label Agency Partners

- GHL agencies, marketing agencies without AI offering
- Kip Hernandez model: ~100 agents deployed through white-label
- Revenue: $50,000–$60,000/month from agents alone

## STEP 2: Getting First Clients — Zero Social Proof Stage

**Framework: Kip Hernandez (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### 30-Day Free Proof-of-Concept Strategy

**Process:**
1. 10–20 targeted local businesses approach karo
2. Offer: Voice AI agent **FREE for 30 days** (client sirf raw usage cost cover kare)
3. Exchange: honest 5-minute Riverside video testimonial upon success

**Conversion:**

**Psychology:** Reciprocity + zero risk = easy yes. Technical mistakes safely karo. Genuine testimonials milti hain.

**Important:** Sirf launch stage ke liye. Jab ek case study aa jaye — stop free work.

## STEP 3: Cold Calling — XeVoice Scripts & System

**Framework: Carmel Hernandez + Shadab Omer (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### Daily Volume Standards

| Caller | Daily Volume |
|--------|------------|
| Malik (early stage, solo) | **200 business owners/day minimum** (Carmel Hernandez) |
| Per SDR (when hired) | **250–300 dials/day** (Shadab Omer) |

**Filter Note (Shadab Omer):** Private owner numbers pe spam filters hote hain — per lead **4 dials** karne pad sakte hain.

### Tonality — "Stumble Scripting" (Carmel Hernandez)

**Rule:** Polish aur speed = robot sound = instant hang-up.

**Execution:**
- Natural pauses: *"and... um..."*
- Slight word stumbles intentionally
- Soft laughs darmiyaan mein
- Slowly aur casually bolo — human authenticity establish karo

### XeVoice Cold Call Opener Script (Carmel Hernandez adapted)

> *"Hi, it's Malik calling from XeVoice. I know I probably caught you in the middle of something, but do you have 20 seconds real quick?"*

> *"So just really quick here — we've been working with home service businesses [in your area/across North America] that are always looking for ways to avoid missing potential client calls, especially after hours. It's just some really cool AI technology. I'm the founder here, and I just wanted to give you a quick call to see if we could set up some time."*

### XeVoice Value Pitch — Competitor Call Recording

**Rule (Carmel Hernandez):** Generic "custom demo" KABHI mat offer karo on cold calls.

**Script:**
> *"I won't show you a generic demo. I'll show you an actual call recording of a [plumber/roofer/HVAC company] in your area where our AI booked the job in 2 minutes."*

Real competitor ki live call = far higher curiosity + engagement.

### Objection Handling Scripts — XeVoice Version

**"I'm driving / busy right now":**
> *"I know you're driving, so I'll be really quick. Can I put a Google Meet invite tentatively in your calendar right now? I just sent it over — if you could just accept that for me while on the phone so I know it's in my calendar as well, then we should be good."*

**"We have dispatchers / don't miss calls":**
> *"That's great you have dispatchers. XeVoice builds AI solutions specifically for after-hours overflow — middle of the night, weekends, when your dispatchers are on other lines. Zero revenue falls through the cracks."*

**"We're part of a franchise":**
> *"That makes sense. Do they handle call center centrally, or do you manage your own local incoming calls? ... We've had many clients come to us from corporate options because it wasn't customized to their specific location. We custom-build from the ground up for your franchise location."*

**Calling Out Inattentive Prospects (Shadab Omer):**
> *"Is this a good time? Because we can always reschedule if I caught you at a bad time. I want your attention because this might change the direction of your business or save you lost revenue."*

**Effect:** Authority flip — 0% se real close chance. Agar waise bhi close nahi hona tha toh risk lene mein kya problem.

## STEP 4: Cold Email System — XeVoice Infrastructure

**Framework: Neha Shafique (Agency Building in 2026) + Jeremy Camilloni (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### Technical Setup — Non-Negotiable (Neha Shafique)

**Rule:** Main `xevoice.io` domain se KABHI cold email mat bhejo.

| Component | XeVoice Setup |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Secondary Domains** | 5 domains purchase karo: `tryxevoice.com`, `getxevoice.com`, `xevoiceai.com` etc. |
| **Email Accounts** | 5 accounts per domain = **25 total sending accounts** |
| **DNS Security** | SPF, DKIM, DMARC + Custom Tracking Domains configure karo |
| **Warmup Protocol** | **2-week mandatory automated warmup** — Instantly.ai se |
| **Sending Limit** | **30 emails/account/day max** = 750–800 total/day |
| **Send Delay** | Randomized **2-minute delay** between consecutive sends |

### Lead Enrichment Pipeline (Neha Shafique)

1. **Scraping:** Google Maps scraper → company URLs + info@ emails
2. **Enrichment:** Apollo API → decision-maker names, personal emails, LinkedIn profiles
3. **Verification:** NeverBounce ya MillionVerifier se run karo
4. **Filter:** Bounce, dead, catch-all, personal (Gmail/Yahoo) discard karo
5. **Segments:**
   - Company campaign → `info@` emails (general)
   - Contact campaign → `hi [FirstName]` personal emails (higher conversion)

### Hyper-Personalized 25/Day Method (Saad Munir — Launch Stage)

**5-Step Flow:**
1. Google Maps/Instant Scraper se local business find karo
2. Website aur assets audit karo — exact gaps identify karo (no call tracking, slow response, no 24/7 coverage)
3. Hyper-personalized email likho — local business name, specific problem, local landmark mention karo
4. Curiosity lead magnet offer: free "Missed Call Revenue Audit" ya competitor call recording
5. 4-sequence automated follow-up trigger karo

**XeVoice Subject Line Formula:**
> *"[Owner Name] — missed calls cost [City] plumbers $9K/mo"*
> *"[Business Name] — after-hours calls you're missing"*

**XeVoice Email Script:**
> *"Hi [Name], I noticed [Business Name] doesn't have any after-hours call coverage. For a [plumbing/HVAC] business in [City], that typically means 40–50 missed calls per month.*
>
> *At $200 average job — that's $8,000–$10,000/month in revenue going to your competitors.*
>
> *I'm Malik from XeVoice. We build custom AI receptionists that answer 24/7, book jobs automatically, and push every lead to your CRM — so you never miss another call.*
>
> *I made a short recording of how our AI handled a call for a [plumber] in [similar city]. Want me to send it over?*
>
> *— Malik, Founder @ XeVoice"*

### 4-Sequence Branching Automation (Saad Munir)

| Sequence | Trigger | Action |
|----------|---------|--------|
| **A — Unopened** | Email not opened | New subject line se resend |
| **B — Opened, No Click** | Opened but no reply | Different angle — competitor's missed call story |
| **C — Clicked Link** | Clicked demo link | Direct: "Book a 15-min call" |
| **D — Downloaded Asset** | Downloaded audit PDF | Automated conversion loop → book call |

### "Pathan Shopkeeper" Follow-Up Rule (Neha Shafique)

**NEVER "Lazy Bumps":**
- ❌ "Just bumping this in your inbox"
- ❌ "Only 10 hours left!" (fake urgency)

**ALWAYS new angle:**
- Follow-up 1: Different pain point (after-hours → weekend coverage)
- Follow-up 2: Lower friction offer (free audit instead of retainer)
- Follow-up 3: Social proof (case study result)
- Follow-up 4: Break-up email ("Should I close your file?")

**Timing:** Monday 7:00 AM–9:00 AM recipient local time se start karo.

### Mass Cold Email Scale (Jeremy Camilloni — Growth Stage)

- **Volume:** **2,000 emails/day** via Mail Reef
- **Scale target:** **10,000 emails/day**
- **Email contains:** XeVoice live demo phone number (calendar link nahi)
- Cold email → prospect demo number dial kare → caller ID capture → immediate callback

**XeVoice Demo Callback Script (Jeremy Camilloni adapted):**
> *"Hey [Name], I saw you had a 3-minute call with the XeVoice demo. I'm Malik, the founder — I call everyone who tests the demo just to get feedback so we can improve the technology. What did you think?"*

**Psychology:** "Feedback lena" frame = sales resistance zero → smoothly custom booking conversation mein transition.

## STEP 10: Pre-Call Nurture & Appointment Management

**Framework: Kip Hernandez + Carmel Hernandez (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### Automated Pre-Call Email Sequence (Kip Hernandez)

Jab prospect calendar pe meeting book kare → auto-trigger:

| Email | Timing | Content |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Email 1 | Immediately | Welcome + Malik founder intro Loom video |
| Email 2 | 24 hrs before | Client video testimonial |
| Email 3 | 2 hrs before | Case study: "23 extra bookings in first month" |

**Goal:** Reduce no-shows + pre-sell before call + build excitement.

### Two-Call Close Appointment Structure (Carmel Hernandez)

**Call 1 — Discovery (1 Hour):**
- Pain points diagnose karo
- Live competitor call recordings play karo
- Price KABHI mat batao: *"Everything is custom-built — I'll consult our technical team."*

**Call 2 — Custom Proposal (1 Hour):**
- Their exact words use karo
- Calculated revenue loss dikhao
- 3-tier tailored options present karo

**Why 2 calls work:**
*"Someone needs about 7 hours of interaction before purchasing. Two calls + email sequence + testimonial videos gets them there."* — Carmel Hernandez
