## 1. Core Hiring Philosophy & Founder Rules

**Source: Arham Abid, Shadab Omer (AI Voice Agency Resources) + Saad Munir, Furqan Aziz (Agency Building in 2026)**

### "Master the Skill Yourself First" Rule (Shadab Omer)

**Core Principle:** Never outsource any technical or sales skill to contractors or employees unless you already master it yourself.

**Why it matters:**
- When you understand the granular mechanics ("ones and zeros"), contractors CANNOT submit fabricated progress reports
- You can set accurate expectations, manage timelines, communicate precisely
- If you outsource GHL automations without knowing GHL → contractor can tell you anything → you'll believe it
- *"The only way to outsource anything is if YOU first know how to do it."*

### "Hire Beginners, Train Internally" Principle (Arham Abid)

**The Anti-Expert Rule:**
- Strongly opposes hiring expensive, highly-trained external executives for core leadership positions
- External experts: *"They will either make you or break you — and if they break you, you cannot afford that."*
- High-level external hires often waste time changing established systems without owning the agency's origin story

**Execution:**
- Hire entry-level candidates with high ambition and teachability
- Train them internally on your specific SOPs from scratch
- They respect the culture, appreciate how the business grew, stay loyal long-term

**Arham Abid's Own Team:**
- CFO: trained from scratch — "she had no idea what a Google Sheet was when I hired her"
- CMO, CEO of sub-brands: all started at basic level
- *"I don't believe in hiring highly trained people."*

### Core Leadership = Internal Promotions Only (Arham Abid)

**Rule:** Core executive positions (CEO, CMO, CFO) must be reserved strictly for team members who started at the bottom and grew alongside the company.

**Exception:** For specific technical specialist roles (not core leadership), external experts can be brought in to execute a defined task — e.g., "build this specific funnel" — not to own a department.

### Founder as Lead Salesperson — Always (Arham Abid)

*"The single biggest salesperson in my company is me. You cannot rely on someone else to give you business. You have to produce your own business."*

- Arham: personally leads sales + 17 internal sales reps
- CEO who doesn't sell = agency that depends on others = fragile business

### Fast Decision-Making as Core Founder Trait (Carmel Hernandez)

The single defining trait of a successful agency CEO: **rapid decisions + gut instinct trust.**

**Carmel's execution examples:**
- Wanted to start coaching program → live within 2 weeks
- Wanted to learn Meta ads → found marketing consultant within 1 week, paid them $3,000/month for 3 months
- Needed developer → posted on social media → **600+ applicants** → hired full-time developer within 1 week

*"If you stay still, your bottlenecks will simply not be fixed and you won't be able to grow."*

## 2. Agency Hierarchy & Org Structure

**Source: Saad Munir, Furqan Aziz (Agency Building in 2026) + Arham Abid (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### Complete Agency Hierarchy — Revenue Stage Mapped (Saad Munir)

**$0 — $5,000/month: Solo Solopreneur**
- Founder = CEO + Lead Salesperson + Delivery
- No hires yet — first close cash, then think about team

**$5,000 — $15,000/month: First Hire**
- **Hire #1: Operations Manager / Project Manager** (NOT a salesperson)
- Rationale: Founder must stay on sales. Ops Manager frees founder's time from execution and client management
- Founder still does all sales personally

**$15,000 — $50,000/month: Sales Team Add**
- SDRs (appointment setters) hired
- First Closer added (or Utah-based part-time closer)
- Content/Marketing execution team

**$50,000+/month: Full Hierarchy**
- CEO → Operations Manager → Department Heads
- Departments: Sales & Marketing | Service Delivery | Client Success | HR/Finance/Admin

### Executive Layer Roles (Saad Munir + Arham Abid)

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| **CEO/Founder** | Vision, strategy, sales, fundraising, key partnerships |
| **Operations Manager** | Execution, delivery, team management, client health |
| **Executive Assistant (EA)** | Single point of contact between CEO and all implementers |
| **CFO / Finance Manager** | Books, payroll, internal financial requests, salary disputes |
| **CMO** | Scaling proven offers — media buying, content scaling |
| **CPO** | Product/offer development |
| **HR Manager** | Team hiring, contracts, disputes, offboarding |

### EA Single Point of Contact Model (Arham Abid)

- CEO communicates with **ONE Executive Assistant only** for all implementation
- EA knows: which funnel designer matches CEO's style, which video editor, which graphic designer
- EA translates CEO strategy → execution tasks → assigns to appropriate team members
- CEO never talks to 6–7 individual implementers directly

*"If I talked to 6-7 people, I wouldn't have time to explain to one, review the funnel, explain to the next, review the video. I trained ONE person — she takes care of everything."*

### CFO Boundary Shielding (Arham Abid)

**Why needed at scale (300–350 team):**
- Large Pakistan-based teams bring constant personal financial requests — salary advances, family loans, personal emergencies
- If founder is the front face → emotional "murawat" (courtesy) forces poor financial decisions
- Finance manager as front face → says "no budget" without emotional damage

*"If someone comes to me, I'll say 'give it to them' out of murawat. My CFO says 'budget nahi hai.'"*

**CFO Scope:**
- Manages payroll, salary disbursements, advance requests, loans
- Handles payment delays (Stripe pauses, bank issues)
- Shields CEO from petty financial management

## 3. Sales Team Architecture & Roles

**Source: Saad Munir, Furqan Aziz, Afroz Khan (Agency Building in 2026) + Arham Abid, Jeremy Camilloni, Shadab Omer (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### SDR vs. Closer Separation Rule (Saad Munir + Furqan Aziz)

| Role | Focus | Compensation |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **SDR (Sales Dev Rep)** | High-volume cold outreach (calls, LinkedIn, email) → qualify → book appointments | Base salary + per-booked-appointment bonus |
| **Closer** | 30-min discovery + closing calls only | Base + sliding scale commission |

**Why separate:** SDR task = volume. Closer task = conversion. Mixing them = both suffer.

### Elevated Titles Strategy (Furqan Aziz)

- Sales team members ko high-authority titles do: *Director of Business Development, VP of Engineering, Head of Growth*
- Prospect psychology: prefer negotiating with high-level executives
- Costs nothing, adds massive perceived credibility

**CEO Guest Appearance Hack (Furqan Aziz):**
BD rep majority of call karta hai → CEO introduce karo for 5–10 minutes:
> *"I invited our CEO to join for 10 minutes because he was excited about your project."*

Instant high perceived value.

### Sliding Scale Commission Structure (Furqan Aziz)

| Deal Size | Commission |
|-----------|-----------|
| Small/entry projects | 3–5% |
| Mid-ticket retainers | 7–10% |
| $1M+ enterprise deals | Up to 20% |

**Why sliding scale:** Incentivize closers to pursue high-ticket enterprise deals, not just easy low-ticket closes.

### Utah Closer Model (Furqan Aziz)

- **Profile:** US-based part-time closers
- **Base:** $1,500–$2,000/month part-time
- **Capacity:** 15–20 closing calls/week
- **Commission:** Sliding scale on top of base

### 4-Man Appointment Setter Team Model (Shadab Omer)

- **Setup:** 4 appointment setters → **100 appointments/month** at peak
- **Practice ratio:** 1 setter = handles lead flow for **2 medical practices** comfortably
- Setters can also do part-time prospecting for Shadab's own pipeline

### Nearshore SDR Hiring Strategy (Jeremy Camilloni)

**Target profile:** Nearshore contractors with clean, native-sounding English accents

**Why nearshore:**
- US small business owners hang up immediately on heavy offshore accents
- Currency exchange rate = attractive incentive for nearshore reps at low cost to agency

**Anti-commission-only rule:**
- High-ticket commission-only closers don't fit $95–$500/month voice AI offer
- Unit economics don't support high per-sale commissions at this ticket size

### Student Commission-Based Callers (Carmel Hernandez)

- Hire university students on commission-per-booked-meeting or low hourly rate during summer
- Scale outreach volume without heavy fixed payroll
- *"You can definitely go that route if you want to up the volume."*

### Internal Sales Team — 17 Reps Model (Arham Abid)

- 17 internal sales reps across different brand verticals
- Arham personally = #1 closer for all brands
- Each sub-company has its own sales team embedded in internal 70-person structure

## 4. Technical & Fulfillment Team

**Source: Saad Munir, Furqan Aziz (Agency Building in 2026) + Carmel Hernandez, Jeremy Camilloni, Kip Hernandez (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### Founder-Led Fulfillment Mastery Before Outsourcing (Carmel Hernandez)

*"You or a co-founder MUST know how to build the agents yourselves, how to set up integrations, test them properly, and handle onboarding before outsourcing to external developers."*

**Why:**
- If fulfillment and customer service are good → customers will not leave
- Can't manage what you don't understand

### Full-Time Developer Hiring Trigger (Carmel Hernandez)

**When to hire:** Fulfillment demand surges beyond co-founder capacity.

**How:**
- Simple social media post → Carmel got **600+ applicants**
- Hired full-time developer within **1 week**

### Technical Co-Founder Model (Jeremy Camilloni)

- Jeremy: cold calling, sales, growth (CEO/Sales role)
- Co-founder: cloud engineer + data engineer
- Backend: AWS infrastructure, Vapi orchestration, custom API connections
- *"I'm fortunate to have a co-founder who helps with the technology side."*

### $1,000 Net Profit Per Resource Rule (Furqan Aziz)

- Every team member must generate **$1,000 net profit/month** after their salary and overhead
- 12-person dev team → must generate $12,000+ monthly net profit
- If not meeting this → rates need to go up, not team size down

### Hourly Rate Increment Sequence (Furqan Aziz)

$8/hr → $10/hr → $12/hr → $15/hr → $20/hr → $25–$30/hr

- Raise rates on every NEW prospect, not existing clients
- Extra margin goes to company reserves, NOT personal spending

## 5. Team Size, Scale & Remote vs. Physical Office

**Source: Arham Abid, Saad Munir (AI Voice Agency Resources + Agency Building in 2026)**

### "Physical Office = Biggest Mistake" (Arham Abid)

*"Building a physical office was my single biggest agency mistake and I still regret it."*

**Costs of physical office:**
- Rent, utilities, furniture, tea, coasters, every small thing
- All capital destroyed that should be reinvested in growth

**350-Person Fully Remote Model (Arham Abid):**
- Total: 350 people, all virtual
- **Internal Core Team (70 people):** Managers, CMO, CPO, CFO, CTO, CEO, EA, 17 sales reps — run internal agency brands (Rozi Academy, Star Boys, Spark Pivo, X3 Revenue)
- **Placed Resources (~280 people):** Trained system experts placed directly with international clients in North America, UK, Europe

### Multi-Profile LinkedIn SDR Engine — 150+ Profiles (Furqan Aziz — InvoZone)

| Component | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Total profiles | 150+ managed LinkedIn profiles |
| Management ratio | 1 BD/SDR manages 5 profiles |
| Acquisition | Authentic profiles rent/borrow from family, friends, staff |
| Rental rate | $10–$30/month ($20 standard, $30 for aged profiles 8K–10K connections) |
| Compliance | Profile's registered name + DOB must match owner's CNIC |
| IP rule | 1 profile per IP/machine |

### $1M ARR LinkedIn Virtual SDR Engine — 100+ Profiles (Jeremy Camilloni)

- Built **$1M ARR in under 1 year** purely through LinkedIn
- Created **100+ LinkedIn accounts** with AI-generated persona headshots + dedicated emails
- **Separate scraper accounts** — Sales Navigator data pulled without risking outreach profiles
- **Centralized operator:** 1 contractor, 8 hours/day, managing ALL incoming responses across 100 profiles from one unified dashboard

## 6. Team Management, Culture & Accountability

**Source: Arham Abid, Saad Munir (Agency Building in 2026 + AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### Firefighter Mindset — Daily Operational Reality (Arham Abid)

*"We wake up every morning going to war to solve operational chaos. That's our job — solving problems."*

- Every day bring new fires: team issues, client problems, payment delays, bad hires
- Accept this as the core job — not an interruption to the real work

### Accountability Coaching System (Arham Abid)

**For community members:**
- Arham acts as aggressive daily accountability coach
- Follows up personally every 2 days: "Where is the offer? Where is the funnel? Where is the sales page?"

**For himself:**
- Uses **Claude AI as personal accountability coach**
- Sets daily to-do list with Claude every morning
- Claude checks up every night: "What happened with today's to-do list?"
- *"I know Claude will ask me at night — I'd be embarrassed to say 'I didn't do it.'"*

### Weekend Disconnect SLA (Arham Abid)

- Monday–Friday: 12–14 hours/day grind
- **Saturday + Sunday: 100% strict disconnect, no compromise whatsoever**
- Goes into mountains/nature, fully offline
- *"Even if millions of dollars are at stake on the weekend — mein nahi karta."*

### Pakistan Team Management Realities (Arham Abid)

**Hard truths shared openly:**

1. **Talent Quality:** Both excellent and terrible talent exists — filtering is essential
2. **Contract Violations:** Employees sign legal NDAs/contracts but sometimes break them, quit without notice, block HR, attempt to poach clients directly
3. **Legal Action:** Technically possible, practically waste of time and energy
4. **CEO Rule:** *"Move on. Protect top performers, cut losses, don't waste months fighting bad actors in court."*

**HR Department Trigger:**
- Once team reaches scale, dedicated HR manager handles contracts, disputes, offboarding — CEO stays out entirely

### Communication Stack — Strict Separation (Arham Abid)

| Channel | Use |
|---------|-----|
| **Slack + ClickUp** | Client-facing: project updates, deliverables, feedback |
| **ClickUp + WhatsApp** | Internal team: task management, daily check-ins |

*"Someone laughed at me that I run a 350-person company on WhatsApp internally. So what? I message, I get a reply."*

## 7. Compensation, Salaries & Tool Stack

**Source: Saad Munir, Furqan Aziz, Afroz Khan (Agency Building in 2026) + Shadab Omer (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### Salaried Founder Model (Saad Munir)

| Revenue | Founder Salary |
|---------|---------------|
| $0–$5,000/mo | $0 — reinvest everything |
| $5,000/mo | $1,000/mo founder salary maximum |
| Scale from there | Based on $5K revenue = $1K salary ratio |

### Capital Allocation Order — Non-Negotiable (Saad Munir)

1. Operating expenses + team salaries
2. Financial reserves (6 months minimum)
3. Founder's baseline salary
4. Quarterly/annual dividends (only when profitable)

**Never:** Personal luxury items from company revenue before this order is followed.

### Sales Team Dialing Stack

| Situation | Tool | Cost |
|-----------|------|------|
| Solo cold caller | **Zoom Phone** | $5/month — unlimited US calling + recording |
| Team (5–6 people) | **GoHighLevel (GHL)** | Multi-line dialing + pipeline |
| Preferred team CRM | **Close.com** | Better speed than GHL for calling |

### Daily Volume Benchmarks

| Role | Daily Volume |
|------|-------------|
| Cold caller (GMB numbers) | 250 dials/day |
| Cold caller (private owner numbers) | 250–300 dials/day (some leads need 4 dials due to spam filters) |
| LinkedIn outreach per profile | 20–25 connection requests/day |
| LinkedIn InMail (Open Profiles) | Up to 100/day free |

## 8. Outsourcing, Legal & Compliance

**Source: Saad Munir, Furqan Aziz (Agency Building in 2026) + Arham Abid (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### Offshore vs. Local Client Rules (Saad Munir)

- Local Pakistani clients: endless unpaid consultation, no advance payment, treat service providers as employees
- **Rule:** Foreign market clients → strict contracts, advance payments always

### Contract & NDA Infrastructure (Arham Abid)

- All employees sign legal NDAs + contracts via lawyers
- Despite this: some break contracts, quit without notice, go directly to clients
- **CEO Response:** Don't waste months chasing bad actors. Move on. HR handles it.

### Multi-Profile LinkedIn Profile Legal Note (Furqan Aziz)

- LinkedIn technically prohibits automated mass outreach
- Jeremy confirmed it still works at scale — platforms haven't fully stopped it
- Agency founder needs to weigh ToS risk vs. growth opportunity
- Separate scraper accounts from outreach accounts to protect primary profiles

## 9. Strategic Partnerships as Team Extension

**Source: Kip Hernandez, Arham Abid (AI Voice Agency Resources) + Saad Munir (Agency Building in 2026)**

### White-Label Fulfillment Partnership (Kip Hernandez)

- Kip's agency serves as white-label Voice AI fulfillment partner for established marketing + GHL agencies
- Partner agencies handle client acquisition; Kip's team handles all technical backend
- Currently: **~100 AI agents deployed** through this model
- Revenue: **$50,000–$60,000/month** from AI agents alone

### Partner-Led White-Label Model (Arham Abid)

1. Identify established Western niche coach/influencer (e.g., roofing coach)
2. Coach = front-facing brand (webinars, content, Meta ads, conferences)
3. Arham's team = backend fulfillment engine under equity or revenue-share agreement
4. *"Nobody would know — no one can highlight me in that ad and say this is Arham's ad."*

### Mastermind Pakistan — Instant Team Formation (Arham Abid)

**Community Specs:**
- Platform: Skool
- Size: 2,000–4,000 members max (no larger — too much noise)
- Fee: 2,500 PKR/month

**Key Use Case — Instant Team Formation:**
- Client suddenly needs 40 sales reps or 10 developers urgently
- Old way: scramble for days hiring externally
- New way: post inside community → within **1 hour**: fulfillment team assembled, briefed, launched

*"Within one hour, our fulfillment team is complete."*

**Capital Circulation Principle (Arham Abid):**
- Money circulates INSIDE the community — doesn't leave
- Members cross-sell services to each other (cold callers, developers, GHL specialists, media buyers)
- Community itself becomes a self-sustaining business ecosystem

### "101 Capable People" Community Rule (Arham Abid — from Jason Steelman)

*"If you find 101 capable people, they are worth 500 people individually. But finding 101 truly capable people in Pakistan is extremely difficult because most don't realize their own true potential."*

- A good salesperson doing cold calling = wasting their talent
- Community's job: match people to their actual strength
- Mastermind Pakistan goal: circulate money, teach technology, enable partnerships, launch offers together
