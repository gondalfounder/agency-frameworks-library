## RULE #0 — Sales Psychology: XeVoice ka Core Mindset

**Framework: Afroz Khan + Saad Munir (Agency Building in 2026)**

### Sales = Exchange of Energy (Afroz Khan)

Sales = coercion ya manipulation nahi — yeh **exchange of energy** hai.

- High-ticket deals ($1,000–$5,000+/month) desperation se nahi — **helpful authority** se close hoti hain
- Jab tum "sell karna" band karo aur focus karo diagnosis pe ke prospect fit hai ya nahi — closing rates dramatically increase hoti hain

**XeVoice Rule:** Malik ko "salesperson" ki tarah nahi — **trusted AI specialist** ki tarah behave karna hai jo diagnose karta hai ke client ke missed calls kitna revenue leak kar rahe hain.

### Consultation Call vs. Sales Call (Afroz Khan)

| Type | Definition | XeVoice Application |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Consultation Call** | Free technical advice, problem on-the-spot solve karna | "Let me set up your voice agent right now" — AVOID |
| **Sales Call** | Diagnose → Pain → Cost of Inaction → Close | "Let me show you exactly what you're losing every month" |

**Rule:** Sales call pe consultation mat karo. Diagnose karo, cost calculate karwao, solution pitch karo.

### "Order Taking" Trap (Afroz Khan)

**The Mistake:** Client bolta hai *"I need an AI agent that handles inbound calls and books appointments"* — Malik notes likhne lagta hai jaise restaurant waiter.

**Why this fails:**
- False sense of progress
- Authority destroy ho jaati hai
- Service commoditize ho jaati hai
- Extreme price sensitivity aati hai

**The Fix:** Frame control maintain karo. Diagnostic questions se conversation lead karo. Solution **prescribe** karo — accept nahi karo.

### Friendliness Trap (Shadab Omer)

**Warning:** Prospect ke saath over-friendly ho jaana = "Fake Reality" create ho jaati hai.

- Client "friend" ki tarah like karta hai lekin transact nahi karta
- Favors maangne lagte hain: *"Can you do this extra thing?"*
- Agle calls pe football ki baatein shuru — deal nahi hoti

**Shadab Omer ka Approach:**
*"Main bahut transactional hun — ek acha tareeqe se. Mujhe paise banana hai, lekin aapko bhi zyada paise banana hain. Tab hi mujhe paise milte hain."*

## STEP 1: Pre-Call Preparation & BUNFEM Qualification

**Framework: Afroz Khan + Saad Munir + Kip Hernandez**

### BUNFEM Qualification Checklist

Malik ka time valuable hai. Har prospect ko is filter se guzaro pehle:

| Letter | Stands For | XeVoice Check |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| **B** | Budget | $350–$1,500/month retainer afford kar sakte hain? |
| **U** | Urgency | Missed calls abhi bhi problem hai? Peak season aa raha hai? |
| **N** | Need | Clear quantifiable missed call / booking gap exist karta hai? |
| **F** | Fit | Local service business with 100+ Google reviews? |
| **E** | Expectations | 14-day setup timeline realistic lagta hai unhe? |
| **M** | Meeting | Next step calendar pe lock karo — call chhorne se pehle |

**Minimum Qualification Benchmarks:**
- 100+ Google reviews (established business)
- Multiple CSRs or dispatchers (call volume hai)
- Business revenue $500K+ ARR

### Pre-Call Assets — 24–48 Hours Before Call (Afroz Khan + Saad Munir)

Bhejo:
- Short **Loom VSL** — 2 minutes, Malik camera pe: "Here's what XeVoice did for a plumber in [City]"
- **Case Study PDF:** "23 extra bookings in first month"
- **Pre-call GHL page** with client video testimonials embedded

**Purpose:** Basic questions answer ho jaate hain, authority establish, prospect enters pre-sold.

### Pre-Call Email Nurture Sequence (Kip Hernandez)

Jab prospect calendar pe book kare → auto-trigger:

| Email | Timing | Content |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Email 1 | Immediately | Welcome + Malik's 2-min intro Loom |
| Email 2 | 24 hrs before | Client video testimonial |
| Email 3 | 2 hrs before | "23 extra bookings in first month" case study |

## STEP 2: Complete 7-Stage XeVoice Sales Call Architecture

**Framework: Afroz Khan + Saad Munir — drawing from Alex Hormozi, Jeremy Miner's NEPQ, Eli Wilde**

| Stage | Duration | Focus |
|-------|---------|-------|
| **Stage 1** | 2–3 mins | Rapport Building |
| **Stage 2** | 2 mins | Frame Setting + Agenda |
| **Stage 3** | 15–20 mins | Discovery (60/40 Rule) |
| **Stage 4** | 5 mins | Deep Discovery + Pain Amplification + Loss Calculation |
| **Stage 5** | 5 mins | Selling the Dream Outcome |
| **Stage 6** | Max 10 mins | XeVoice Unique Mechanism Pitch |
| **Stage 7** | 10 mins | 1-to-10 Framework + Price Drop + Silence |

---

### Stage 1: Rapport Building (2–3 Minutes)

- Prospect ki energy se match karo immediately
- Pre-call research: LinkedIn, Google Maps listing, recent posts
- Authentic relatability point dhundo — unki city, their industry, recent news
- Artificial small talk AVOID: "How's the weather?" — nahi

**Opener Example:**
> *"Hey [Name], great to connect. I saw you've got an impressive operation — [X] Google reviews and [Y] years in [City]. Appreciate you taking the time."*

---

### Stage 2: Frame Setting + Agenda (2 Minutes)

**Exact Script (Afroz Khan — XeVoice adapted):**
> *"Hey [Name], this call is scheduled for about [X] minutes. We're going to look at what's specifically happening with your incoming calls — what's working, what's leaking revenue — and see whether we're the right fit for each other. If at the end I feel like we can genuinely help you capture more jobs, we'll discuss how we can move forward today. Does that sound fair?"*

**Why this works:** Plants early decision-making seed. At the end of the call, prospect already mentally prepared to decide.

---

### Stage 3: Discovery — The 60/40 Rule (15–20 Minutes)

**60/40 Rule (Afroz Khan):**
- Prospect: **60% bole**
- Malik: **40% ya kam bole**
- Person jo khud bolta hai — usse zyada samajh aati hai + pain internalize hota hai

**XeVoice Discovery Questions:**

> *"What was the primary reason you booked this call today?"*

> *"How are you currently handling calls that come in after hours or when your team is busy?"*

> *"How long has this been an issue for your business?"*

> *"Have you tried anything to solve this before — an answering service, a receptionist, anything like that?"*

> *"Where are you right now in terms of call volume, and where do you want to get to in the next 6 months?"*

**Key Goal:** Uncover: call volume, peak hours, after-hours gaps, existing solutions that failed.

---

### Stage 4: Deep Discovery + Pain Amplification + Financial Loss Calculation

**XeVoice Loss Calculation — Live on Call (Afroz Khan adapted):**

> *"You mentioned you miss calls during peak times and after hours. Have you ever thought about how many calls that actually is per day?"*

Prospect: *"Maybe 7–8 calls a day."*

> *"If even 5 of those 8 people would have booked a job — and your average job ticket is $200 — that's $1,000 in lost revenue every single day. Over a month, that's $30,000 gone. $360,000 per year going to your competitors, just because the phone wasn't answered."*

**Rule:** Do this calculation LIVE on the call. Let them see the math. The number hits differently when they watch it add up.

---

### Stage 5: Selling the Dream Outcome (5 Minutes)

**Rule (Afroz Khan):** KABHI MAT BECHO:
- "AI Voice Agents"
- "Vapi integrations"
- "Prompt engineering"
- "CRM webhooks"

**HAMESHA BECHO:**
- More jobs booked
- Zero missed calls
- Revenue captured while you sleep
- Competitors losing leads that come to you

**XeVoice Dream Pitch:**
> *"Imagine waking up tomorrow morning and seeing 3 new jobs already booked from calls that came in at 2 AM — while you were asleep. Your AI receptionist answered, got their details, checked your calendar, and booked them in. No call center. No human. Just jobs showing up."*

**VSL Framework: Vision → Friction → Lift (Afroz Khan)**
- **Vision:** Paint the dream (above)
- **Friction:** "But right now, that's not happening because..."
- **Lift:** "XeVoice's Revenue Capture System fixes exactly that."

---

### Stage 6: The XeVoice Pitch — Unique Mechanism (Max 10 Minutes)

**Rule:** Presentation max 10 minutes. No 23-page pitch deck. Each point = micro-case study.

**XeVoice Unique Mechanism:**
> *"What we do is called the XeVoice 3-Step Revenue Capture System: Detect → Deploy → Dominate.*
>
> *Step 1 — Detect: We audit your exact call flow and identify every gap where revenue is leaking.*
>
> *Step 2 — Deploy: We build a custom AI receptionist trained in YOUR brand, YOUR services, YOUR schedule — live within 14 days.*
>
> *Step 3 — Dominate: We monitor every call, optimize the prompts weekly, and ensure your AI is outperforming your competitors 24/7."*

**Connect Each Point to Proof:**
- Show a screenshot of call logs from existing client
- Play 30-second clip of voice agent booking a job
- Show monthly booking increase chart

---

### Stage 7: 1-to-10 Framework + Price Drop + Silence

**Framework: Alex Hormozi (Referenced by Afroz Khan + Saad Munir)**

Price batane se PEHLE — yeh zaroor run karo:

**Step 1 — The Question:**
> *"Based on everything we've covered today — on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 means this is complete nonsense and 10 means this is exactly what your business needs — how close does the XeVoice system feel to solving your missed call problem?"*

**Step 2 — Counter-Intuitive Follow-Up:**
Prospect "8" bole → **"Why not a 1?"** — NOT "Why not a 10?"

**Why "Why not a 1?":**
Forces them to list every single benefit themselves → self-sells → deep psychological commitment before price drop.

**Step 3 — Isolate the Gap:**
> *"What would it take to turn that 8 into a 10?"*

Hidden objections surface BEFORE price. Resolve them NOW.

**Step 4 — Price Drop:**
> *"The investment to take you from where you are right now — losing $30,000/month to missed calls — to where you want to be — capturing every job, 24/7 — is [Tier X]."*

Present 3-tier table (Kickstart / Growth / Dominate).

**Step 5 — COMPLETE SILENCE.**
Pick up water glass. Look at screen. Do NOT speak for **5–10 seconds.** Let them process.

## STEP 3: Two-Call Close Framework

**Framework: Carmel Hernandez (AI Voice Agency Resources)**

### Call 1 — Discovery + Live Proof (1 Hour)

**Focus:** Diagnose pain, calculate loss, build trust.

**What to do:**
- Run Stages 1–5 from above
- Play live competitor call recordings (NOT a generic demo)
- *"I won't show you a generic demo. I'll play you an actual call of a [plumber] in [their city] where our AI booked a $600 job in 2 minutes."*

**Price:** KABHI MAT BATAO.
> *"Everything we build is fully custom — I need to consult our technical team and create a tailored proposal for your specific setup."*

**Why:** No reason for them to back out — they don't know the price. Keeps Call 2 mandatory.

### Call 2 — Custom Proposal Presentation (1 Hour)

**Preparation:**
- Their exact words from Call 1 use karo
- Calculated revenue loss dikhao (their numbers)
- 3 tailored options: Kickstart / Growth / Dominate

**Opening:**
> *"Based on everything you shared last time — you're losing about 40 calls per week at $200 average job — I've put together three options specifically for your business."*

**Why 2 Calls Work (Carmel Hernandez):**
*"Someone needs about 7 hours of interaction before purchasing a high-ticket service. Two calls + email sequence + testimonial videos gets them there."*

## STEP 4: Objection Handling — Complete Scripts

**Framework: Afroz Khan + Saad Munir + Carmel Hernandez + Shadab Omer**

### "I Need to Think About It"

**Script (Afroz Khan):**
> *"I completely understand. Usually when people say they need to think about it, it means they either aren't completely certain about the system, or the investment feels out of reach. Which one is it for you?"*

**Then — Cost of Inaction Frame:**
> *"You mentioned earlier that you're losing roughly 40 calls per week × $200 = $8,000/month in missed revenue. That's $96,000/year. Compared to that, a $500/month investment with XeVoice is minimal. Is delaying this another month worth losing another $8,000?"*

**Master Anchor Line (Afroz Khan):**
> *"The cost of inaction is always higher than the cost of action."*

*Saad Munir: "Yeh sentence mera favorite hai — mein 400 calls mein use kar chuka hun."*

### "I Need to Talk to My Partner / Spouse"

**Prevention during Discovery (Afroz Khan):**
> *"Besides yourself, is there anyone else involved in approving new technology investments for your business?"*

**If objection occurs:**
- NEVER email PDF proposal for them to review alone
- Script: *"That makes total sense. Since your partner hasn't seen the call flow analysis and case studies, let's schedule a quick 15-minute call with both of you. I'll walk through the numbers directly and answer any questions."*

### "Price is Too High / No Budget"

**First — Ask (Afroz Khan):**
> *"Compared to what?"*

Then — if they say "it's too much":
> *"You mentioned you're losing $8,000/month to missed calls. Over 12 months, that's $96,000 walking out the door. XeVoice at $500/month over 12 months is $6,000. You'd spend $6,000 to recover $96,000. Which number feels bigger to you?"*

### "We Already Have a Dispatcher / We Don't Miss Calls"

**Script (Carmel Hernandez adapted for XeVoice):**
> *"That's great you have dispatchers. XeVoice is specifically built for after-hours — 11 PM, weekends, when your dispatcher is on another line or off shift. One missed call at 10 PM that books a $500 emergency job pays for XeVoice for an entire month. Does that make sense?"*

### "We Use a Franchise / Corporate System"

**Script (Carmel Hernandez adapted):**
> *"That makes sense. Does your franchise handle incoming calls centrally, or does each location manage its own leads? ... Many of our clients came from franchise systems because the corporate option wasn't customized to their local market. We build specifically for your franchise location — your services, your hours, your pricing."*

### Calling Out Inattentive / Disrespectful Prospects (Shadab Omer)

> *"Is this a good time? We can always reschedule. I want your full attention because what we're about to discuss could either save or cost your business thousands of dollars this month."*

**Effect:** Authority flip. If they were going to close anyway — this seals it. If they weren't — at least you find out now.

## STEP 5: Follow-Up Rules

**Framework: Afroz Khan + Saad Munir (Agency Building in 2026)**

### BUNFEM — Non-Negotiable End-of-Call Rule

**Rule:** Koi bhi call KABHI bina BUNFEM ke end mat karo.

- Call pe hi exact date, time, calendar invite lock karo
- *"I'll email you next week"* = deal killer
- Always: *"Before we wrap up — let's lock in the next touchpoint right now. Does [Day] at [Time] work for you?"*

### "No Async Proposal" Rule (Afroz Khan)

- Offline PDF proposals → extremely low close rates
- Proposal zaruri ho → 4 days baad **live 15-minute Proposal Review Call** pe present karo
- *"Proposals sent without a call rarely close."*

### Bait-and-Switch Audit Close (Shadab Omer)

**Used when:** Prospect hesitating pe full retainer close.

> *"Tell you what — let me put together a free Revenue Audit specific to your business. I'll show you exactly which calls you're missing, what they're worth, and how we'd fix it. 10 minutes on Zoom, no pitch. Sound good?"*

Zoom pe → full strategy lay out karo → DFY package mein transition.

**Framework: Alex Hormozi Value Equation (Referenced by Shadab Omer)**

| Tier 1 Starter ($350–$500/mo) | 3–5% |
| Tier 2 Growth ($600–$1,000/mo) | 7–10% |

  > *"I brought in our CEO for a few minutes because he was excited about your project."*
